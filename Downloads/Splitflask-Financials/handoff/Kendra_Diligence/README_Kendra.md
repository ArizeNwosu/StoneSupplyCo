## 2025 GMV Context

The 2025 GMV decline ($751K → $514K) reflects two specific, traceable events:
1. **TikTok wind-down:** TikTok Shop was $155K in 2024; the channel was deliberately wound down due to platform regulatory uncertainty. Amazon and Shopify cores held: Amazon $224K and Shopify $165K.
2. **Entity restructuring:** Operations shifted from SGM/Social Gear Media LLC to SPLT Inc. (new C-corp, January 2025). Revenue landed across two entities mid-year during the transition.

Both factors are documented and visible in the channel-level data in the workbook.

## 2026 / Post-Shark-Tank Context

The Shark Tank episode aired January 2026 (visible demand spike: $32.7K that month). Underlying run-rate Feb–Jun: $13–18K/month. The honest framing is continued DTC normalization post-launch-year. Growth levers not yet reflected in the 2026 run-rate: wholesale, Best Buy/Walmart Mirakl (live Dec 2025), international distribution, and the Kendra Scott partnership itself.

The 2026 run-rate intentionally under-claims. The Kendra Scott partnership is the growth catalyst being funded — projections built off a Shark Tank spike would be discounted by any analyst.
