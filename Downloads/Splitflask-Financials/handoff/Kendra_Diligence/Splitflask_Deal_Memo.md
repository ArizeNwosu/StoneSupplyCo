## The Brand

Splitflask is a patented dual-chamber tumbler (US Patent 12,577,032 B2) that lets users carry two beverages simultaneously — one hot, one cold — in a single insulated vessel. Sold direct-to-consumer and through wholesale retail accounts, across Amazon, Shopify, TikTok Shop, Best Buy, Walmart Marketplace, and B2B distribution channels.

## Three-Year Commercial Trajectory

| Year | Splitflask Tumbler GMV | Status |
|------|----------------------|--------|
| 2024 | **$751,891** | Verified & locked — bank + platform sourced |
| 2025 | **$513,987** | Verified — see narrative below |
| 2026 H1 (Jan–Jun) | **~$106K confirmed core** | Preliminary — platform-sourced, unaudited; Global-e + SG/AU verified (no material add) |
| 2026 Conservative Annualized | **~$213K (straight H1 × 2)** | Labeled projection — see framing note; last-3-mo run-rate $184K |

**2024** was the brand's launch year at scale. Four channels active: Amazon ($321K), Shopify ($189K charged), TikTok Shop ($155K), and Wholesale/B2B ($87K). Consumer demand at list price reached $1,032,079; net of promotional discounts, consumers paid $751,891 (GMV). Total deposits through the business were $895,754 — the figure referenced on air.

**2025** shows a $238K decline in GMV. This requires honest context: it is a transition year, not a demand decline.
- **Entity restructuring:** Splitflask operations moved from SGM/Social Gear Media (2024 entity) to SPLT Inc., a new C-corp formed January 2025. Amazon disbursements switched accounts mid-February; Shopify TIN changed. Revenue landed across two entities mid-year, and the operational shift consumed management bandwidth.
- **TikTok wind-down:** TikTok Shop was a $155K channel in 2024. Platform instability and regulatory uncertainty caused a deliberate wind-down; TikTok contributed only $24K in 2025 and is effectively closed. Amazon and Shopify cores held: Amazon $224K (−30%) and Shopify $165K (−12%) — the structural channels performed.
- Wholesale remained strong at ~$84K, confirming B2B demand.

**2026** shows continued DTC channel normalization. The Shark Tank episode aired January 2026, generating a visible demand spike (January: $32.7K). Stripping that spike, the Feb–Jun underlying run-rate is $13–18K/month. Amazon US H1 annualized ~$161K (vs 2025 $224K); Shopify annualized ~$48K (vs 2025 $165K, reflecting heavy promotional discounting in H1). TikTok is effectively closed. Core-channel annualized run-rate $184–213K vs 2025 $514K — the honest framing is continuing DTC moderation, not re-acceleration. The growth levers not yet reflected in the run-rate: wholesale ($84K in 2025, 2026 status unconfirmed), Best Buy/Walmart Mirakl (live Dec 2025, nascent), and the Kendra Scott distribution itself. International confirmed via B2 true-up: Global-e already inside Shopify export (no add); Amazon SG = $0 in 2026; Amazon AU = one sale (~$48) in Jan; all negligible.

## 2026 Run-Rate — Conservative Framing

The January 2026 spike reflects the Shark Tank airing, not a sustainable baseline. The honest framing:

- **H1 2026 actual: ~$106K confirmed core** — unimpeachable, platform-sourced.
- **Last-3-month run-rate (Apr–Jun):** ~$15.3K/month → **~$184K annualized** (core channels; no wholesale or international).
- **Conservative H1 × 2: ~$213K** — labeled projection, straight-line, not adjusted for acceleration.
- **Upside not baked in:** wholesale ($84K/yr historical), international (nascent), Best Buy/Walmart Mirakl (live Dec 2025), and the Kendra Scott distribution itself are NOT reflected in the run-rate.

The projection under-claims intentionally. The Kendra Scott partnership is the growth catalyst being funded — projections built off a Shark Tank TV spike would be discounted by any analyst. Actual monthly data is in the workbook; the ramp is visible.

## Brand Assets

**US Patent 12,577,032 B2** — Dual-chamber tumbler design, issued. A defensible moat; third-party copies can be challenged.

**Multi-channel presence** — Amazon, Shopify DTC, TikTok Shop (established), Best Buy and Walmart Marketplace (live Dec 2025), international distributors (Australia, Japan, Taiwan, Kuwait) and marketplace platforms (Amazon SG, AU, MX, CA). Distribution infrastructure is built; the SKU and the accounts exist.

**Wholesale / B2B accounts** — ESP (~$57.6K 2024), Von Maur, Knocking, SATS. Reorder history. Retail placement validates the product with physical shelf buyers.

**Verified demand at $1M+ list price** — 2024 list-price demand reached $1,032,079 before promotional discounts. The $280K discount line is a promotional strategy, not pricing weakness; it shows both the scale of demand and the brand's ability to drive volume through strategic promotions.

## What This Document Is and Is Not

This memo and the accompanying workbook cover **Splitflask tumbler commercial performance only** — consumer-paid revenue across the brand's active sales channels. The scope is the asset being acquired: the dual-chamber tumbler and its commercial track record. Other products sold under separate entity accounts are out of scope by definition and do not appear in any figure here.

2024 and 2025 figures are management reconstructions from bank statements and platform source documents, verified and cross-reconciled. 2026 H1 data is preliminary and unaudited — pulled from platform dashboards through June 30, 2026.
