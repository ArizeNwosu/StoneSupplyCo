## ⚠️ THE CENTRAL GUARDRAIL — TWO DENOMINATORS, NEVER ONE

There are two different "revenue" universes in this project. They are different numbers for
different audiences. **A figure is meaningless without stating which universe it belongs to.**

1. **SPLITFLASK GMV (the deal / Kendra universe) — TUMBLER ONLY.**
   This is the asset Kendra is buying. It contains Splitflask tumbler sales and NOTHING else.

2. **TOTAL GROSS RECEIPTS (the tax / amendment universe) — ALL PRODUCTS, ALL ENTITIES.**
   This is the 1099-K-floor denominator for the IRS amendments. It includes the Cyberbackpack
   product line sold through Cyberbrands.

**The bridge between them is Cyberbackpack (~$104,678 in 2024, 1099-K basis).** Cyberbackpack
is a SEPARATE PRODUCT (a backpack, not a tumbler). It COUNTS for tax (it hit a 1099-K floor
and must be reported) and NEVER counts as Splitflask revenue (Kendra is not buying it).

**Each product has its own dedicated payment accounts — no account ever sold both products.**
So a 1099-K, which is per-account/TIN, is ALSO per-product here. Product attribution is an
account-mapping problem, not a SKU problem. The account-to-product map (2024):

| Account | Product | 2024 1099-K (approx, verify to form) |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon main (US+CA+MX, SGM TIN …7502) | **Splitflask** | ~$366,467 |
| TikTok Shop (SGM TIN …7502) | **Splitflask** | ~$155,295 |
| Shopify "Splitflask store" (Personal TIN …7639) | **Splitflask** | ~$164,614 |

Note: "Cyberbrands" = Cyberbackpack. The SGM 1099-K FLOOR ($604,026) mixes products at the
TIN level (it contains the Cyberbrands-store Cyberbackpack $82,264), but each ACCOUNT is
single-product — so tag by account, not by TIN, to build the Splitflask vs Cyberbackpack
split. Splitflask 1099-K total ~$686,376; Cyberbackpack ~$104,678; sum = $791,055 total filed.
The $751,891 Splitflask GMV is CLEAN (never included Cyberbackpack) and STANDS.

- Do NOT fold Cyberbackpack into any figure presented to the investor/deal.
- Do NOT drop Cyberbackpack from the tax-amendment gross receipts.
- When asked for "the 2024 GMV number," ALWAYS clarify which universe. Grabbing the wrong one
  is the single most likely error in this project.
- A prior session mislabeled the tax denominator (~$855K) as "Splitflask GMV." That was
  wrong. $855K is correct FOR TAX. It is NOT Splitflask GMV. This file exists partly to stop

- Surface the separation, don't bury it: be ready to show "total receipts − Cyberbackpack =

## On-air claims — RECONCILE, DO NOT TARGET

The on-air figures below are **reference points and commitments to reconcile against — they
are NOT targets to build the reconstruction up to.** The reconstruction reports what the
source documents prove. Where the honest number falls short of or exceeds an on-air claim,
the three-tier waterfall (below) explains the relationship. Never reverse-engineer figures to
hit an on-air number — a reconstruction built to a target is fragile under diligence; one
built from source is robust.

- **2024 on-air claim: $895,000 (~$895,754.12).** Established basis: this ties to **total
  Splitflask deposits** (cash through the business — all inflows), which reconciles
  penny-exact. It sits ABOVE GMV in the waterfall. It is NOT the GMV number and NOT the tax
  revenue number.
- **2025 on-air claim: $499,000.** Established basis: this ≈ **2025 Splitflask GMV** (~$489K,
  within 2%). For 2025 the on-air claim attaches at the GMV tier, not the deposits tier.

Note the asymmetry: **2024's on-air number is a deposits figure; 2025's is essentially GMV.**
Same waterfall, different tier per year. A session that assumes both attach at the same tier
will misreconcile.

## The three-tier waterfall (the reconciliation mechanism)

Every place an on-air claim appears, reconcile through these tiers, each tied to its own
source:

1. **Total deposits / cash through the business** — all inflows (incl. founder/family loans,
   refunds, transfers, consulting). 2024 ≈ $895,754.12. This is where the 2024 on-air claim
   attaches.
2. **GMV** — what customers actually paid for Splitflask across all channels. 2024 = $751,891;
   2025 ≈ $489,477. This is where the 2025 on-air claim attaches.
3. **Net revenue (tax-reported)** — after platform fees; ties to the (amended) return.
   2024 ≈ $560,938; 2025 ≈ $332,015.

Rationale (already war-gamed): if the package asserts "on-air = GMV," an analyst building GMV
bottom-up from channel exports gets a lower number and it looks reverse-engineered. The
waterfall avoids this by letting each tier tie to its own documented source.
