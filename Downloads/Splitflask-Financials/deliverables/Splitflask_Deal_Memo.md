## The Brand

Splitflask is a patented dual-chamber tumbler (US Patent 12,577,032 B2) that lets users carry two beverages simultaneously — one hot, one cold — in a single insulated vessel. Sold direct-to-consumer and through wholesale retail accounts, across Amazon, Shopify, TikTok Shop, Best Buy, Walmart Marketplace, and B2B distribution channels.

## Three-Year Commercial Trajectory

**GMV basis:** Net Merchandise Sales — customer-paid value, after seller-funded discounts and returns, before platform fees, shipping, and tax. Applied uniformly across all channels and all years. See `GMV_Rebuild_Net_Basis.md` for full channel-by-channel sourcing.

| Year | Splitflask Tumbler GMV | Status |
|------|----------------------|--------|
| 2024 | **$700,134** | Verified & locked — platform source documents, net basis |
| 2025 | **$461,934** | Verified — see narrative below |
| 2026 H1 (Jan–Jun) | **~$98K confirmed core** | Preliminary — platform-sourced, unaudited |
| 2026 Conservative Annualized | **~$196K (straight H1 × 2)** | Labeled projection — see framing note |

**2024** was the brand's launch year at scale. Four channels active: Amazon ($321K net of refunds), Shopify ($166K net of discounts and returns), TikTok Shop ($126K net of seller discounts, excl. shipping), and Wholesale/B2B ($87K). Consumer demand at list price reached $1,032,079; net of promotional discounts and returns, consumers paid $700,134 (GMV). Total deposits through the business were $895,754 — the figure referenced on air.

*Note on prior $751,891 figure: that figure mixed bases across channels — Shopify used an unsourced estimate, TikTok included $29K of shipping revenue. The corrected $700,134 applies one consistent definition. The on-air claim ($895K) still attaches at the deposits tier, which is unchanged.*

**2025** shows a $238K decline in GMV. This requires honest context: it is a transition year, not a demand failure.
- **Entity restructuring:** Splitflask operations moved from SGM/Social Gear Media (2024 entity) to SPLT Inc., a new C-corp formed January 2025. Amazon disbursements switched accounts mid-February; Shopify TIN changed. Revenue landed across two entities mid-year, and the operational shift consumed management bandwidth.
- **TikTok wind-down:** TikTok Shop was a $126K channel in 2024. Platform instability and regulatory uncertainty caused a deliberate wind-down; TikTok contributed only $11K in 2025 and is effectively closed.
- **Structural channels held:** Amazon $206K (−35.9% — settlement basis vs prior 1099-K estimate), Shopify $149K (−10.3% — the structural DTC channel showed modest decline).
- Wholesale remained strong at ~$84K, confirming B2B demand.
- *Note on on-air $499K claim for 2025:* The reconstructed GMV is $462K. The $37K difference (8%) reflects sourcing corrections: TikTok corrected from gross to net (−$13K), Amazon from 1099-K estimate to settlement basis (−$18K), Shopify from working estimate to SKU-export net (−$16K). These are measurement corrections, not missing revenue.

**2026** shows continued DTC channel normalization. The Shark Tank episode aired January 2026, generating a visible demand spike (January: $32.7K). Stripping that spike, the Feb–Jun underlying run-rate is $13–18K/month. Amazon US H1 annualized ~$145K (vs 2025 $206K); Shopify annualized ~$48K (vs 2025 $149K, reflecting heavy promotional discounting in H1 — 48% discount rate). TikTok is effectively closed. Core-channel annualized run-rate ~$196K vs 2025 $462K — the honest framing is continuing DTC moderation, not re-acceleration. The growth levers not yet reflected in the run-rate: wholesale ($84K in 2025, 2026 status unconfirmed), Best Buy/Walmart Mirakl (live Dec 2025, nascent), and the Kendra Scott distribution itself. International confirmed: Global-e already inside Shopify export (no add); Amazon SG/AU negligible in 2026.

## 2026 Run-Rate — Conservative Framing

The January 2026 spike reflects the Shark Tank airing, not a sustainable baseline. The honest framing:

- **H1 2026 actual: ~$98K confirmed core** — platform-sourced, net basis (Amazon $72K + Shopify $24K + TikTok $2K).
- **Last-3-month run-rate (Apr–Jun):** ~$14.2K/month → **~$170K annualized** (core channels; no wholesale or international).
- **Conservative H1 × 2: ~$196K** — labeled projection, straight-line, not adjusted for acceleration.
- **Upside not baked in:** wholesale ($84K/yr historical), international (nascent), Best Buy/Walmart Mirakl (live Dec 2025), and the Kendra Scott distribution itself are NOT reflected in the run-rate.

The projection under-claims intentionally. The Kendra Scott partnership is the growth catalyst being funded — projections built off a Shark Tank TV spike would be discounted by any analyst. Actual monthly data is in the workbook; the ramp is visible.

## Brand Assets

**US Patent 12,577,032 B2** — Dual-chamber tumbler design, issued. A defensible moat; third-party copies can be challenged.

**Multi-channel presence** — Amazon, Shopify DTC, TikTok Shop (established), Best Buy and Walmart Marketplace (live Dec 2025), international distributors (Australia, Japan, Taiwan, Kuwait) and marketplace platforms (Amazon SG, AU, MX, CA). Distribution infrastructure is built; the SKU and the accounts exist.

**Wholesale / B2B accounts** — ESP (~$57.6K 2024), Von Maur, Knocking, SATS. Reorder history. Retail placement validates the product with physical shelf buyers.

**Verified demand at $1M+ list price** — 2024 list-price demand reached $1,032,079 before promotional discounts. The $280K discount line is a promotional strategy, not pricing weakness; it shows both the scale of demand and the brand's ability to drive volume through strategic promotions.
